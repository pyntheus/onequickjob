## One charging path for finishing a visit (app/payments/charging.py, app/admin/tasks.py: L3's)
- What: L2's finish saves the finished visit (the calibration data and the day's mileage) in
  one transaction, then calls `app.payments.charging.charge_visit`. L2's own gateway call,
  ledger entry and messages, its resume task (`provider_resume_charges`) and
  `Charge.attempted_at` are gone. Added to app.payments, following its patterns, only what L3's
  path didn't do yet:
  1. `charging.start_unstarted`: a finished visit whose charge never started (the request
     stopped between saving the visit and `charge_visit` recording its intent) is started, with
     its normal first-attempt key. L3's `settle_pending_payments` task calls it first (one line
     in app/admin/tasks.py), with that task's 5-minute grace and 7-day look-back. Two starts at
     once are refused by `charge_visit`'s guarded intent (409, ignored), so it's charged once.
     Pending charges stay with `settle_unknown`, unchanged. Finishing the visit again also
     starts it (`charge_visit` is idempotent).
  2. In `apply_result`'s success branch (the one transaction that records every successful
     visit charge: the finish, a webhook, the settle task or an admin retry), a one-off booking
     becomes `completed` (state-machines.md: "a one-off's visit is finished and charged") and
     `notices.charged` sends `limit_reached` when the payment takes the provider to their
     earnings limit (once per period and amount). Both used to happen only in L2's finish, so a
     payment confirmed later (3-D Secure, a retry) skipped them.
- Why: brief 2a. One path, so webhooks, retries and the settle task never race two
  implementations.
- Writers: `bookings` is now also written by `app.payments.charging` (status `completed`);
  domain.md's writers line for bookings should say so.
- Breaking? No. (`tests/provider/test_finish.py`: `test_a_charge_that_never_started_is_started_once`,
  `test_finishing_again_starts_a_charge_that_never_started`,
  `test_a_payment_that_lands_later_completes_the_one_off_and_counts_to_the_limit`.)

# Part 2. Open

## Re-seeding leaves money records from demo runs behind (app/seed/cleanup.py)
- What: `clear_children_of_bookings` deletes visits under a cleared booking but not their
  `ledger_entries`, `mileage_logs` (whose `visit_ids` point at them), ratings or files. Time off
  booked by seeded providers, and the cover requests it made, also survive `make seed`, so a second
  demo run finds the week already booked off. Please delete ledger entries and mileage logs for the
  cleared visits, and `time_off` plus cover `job_requests` (`cover_for_visit_id` on a cleared or
  seeded visit) for seeded providers.
- Why: after a demo finish and `make seed`, the tax pack and earnings still include the orphaned
  £31 charge, and the next acceptance walk fails on "You've already got time off booked".
- Workaround now: none in code (F's file). The tax pack copes with a ledger entry whose visit has
  gone (blank job name).
- Breaking? No.

- What: guard the visit update in `_cover` on `status: "scheduled"` as well as the cover state.

- Breaking? No.

## Helpers and the shared offer endpoints (app/shared/marketplace_routes.py, app/core/deps.py)
- Decided (Hasan, at the rebase): helpers never accept, counter or price; they only do visits.
- What: refuse helpers on `POST /api/p/requests/{ref}/accept|counter` (and decide what
  `helper_of` is for: `current_provider` gives any user with it the provider's context).
- Why: a helper could accept jobs or suggest prices on the provider's behalf (Codex review, high).
  This is the auth model, so it's an open question in the L2 report.
- Workaround now: helpers added in the app (`POST /api/p/helpers`) are created with **no**
  `helper_of` and no roles, so `current_provider` and the shared offer endpoints refuse them; the
  provider app finds them through the provider's helper list (`app/provider/acting.py`). Only a
  user who already has `helper_of` (the seed's Tom) can still reach the shared offer endpoints.
- Breaking? No. (`helper_of` keeps working for existing helpers.)

## Seed Tom's documents (app/seed, people.json)
- What: give the seeded helper Tom verified documents on his helper entry

## L3: verifying renewals and helpers' documents
- What: (1) a provider may hold a verified, in-date document **and** a pending renewal of the
  same type (uploaded before the old one runs out, so they keep working). L3's verify and reject
  act on the first document of a type (`admin/views.doc_of`), and `Providers.set_document`
  replaces every document of that type. Checked at the rebase: verifying re-verified the old
  copy and silently dropped the renewal. L2 now stores an upload first, so admins see and
  verify the renewal (`test_an_admin_checking_a_renewal_checks_the_upload_not_the_old_copy`).
  Still wrong: rejecting a renewal also replaces the in-date verified copy, so the provider
  can't take jobs until they upload again, and the admin's list flags their insurance while a
  renewal waits. Please make reject keep the verified copy, and show both in the admin view.
  (2) A helper's documents are on `providers.helpers[].documents`, uploaded through
  `POST /api/p/documents` signed in as the helper (the helper goes invited -> checking on their
  first upload). L3 can't verify them or set a helper `ready` yet.
- Why: A3 renewals (Lorna's DBS in the reminder window) and helpers' checks.
- Breaking? No (fields are additive: `Helper.documents`, default empty).
